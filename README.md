## 03 / Where technology meets reality

### Research focus — Mobility in Sousse

**Can data help us understand how our cities move?**

One of my research interests focuses on shared-taxi transportation (Louage) in Sousse, Tunisia.

I am exploring how transportation patterns, passenger flows, and mobility demand can be studied through data.

The long-term direction is to investigate how Data Science and Machine Learning could contribute to demand prediction and smarter mobility solutions.

**What interests me about this project:**

- Starting with an actual local challenge.
- Understanding the system before proposing solutions.
- Collecting and analyzing meaningful data.
- Exploring predictive approaches and their potential applications.
