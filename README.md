### 03 / THE WORLD IS MY LABORATORY

**A project that reflects how I think:**

#### 🚕 Mobility in Sousse
*Local observations. Data exploration. Future possibilities.*

Sousse is more than the place where this research takes place. It's an opportunity to investigate a real-world system that people interact with every day.

My research interest focuses on shared-taxi transportation (Louage), exploring:

- Transportation patterns and passenger flows.
- Mobility demand and its variations.
- The potential of data analysis to reveal hidden patterns.
- Future possibilities for Machine Learning-based demand prediction.

**My question:** Could we understand local transportation better by combining real-world observations with data and intelligent systems?

[↗ Explore Mobility in Sousse — Research Repository](https://github.com/marymaryem/mobility-in-sousse-research)
